ABOUT COMPLAINT BOX

Built for one college, by the people who run it.

Complaint Box is the college's official channel for reporting hostel, mess, academic, transport, library, Wi-Fi, and general campus issues. Every complaint reaches the right department automatically and stays visible to you until it's resolved — log in with your college ID to raise one and follow it through.
